03

License verification

For every candidate, check the Virginia DPOR contractor license by exact business name. Check current status, business identity, and heating/ventilation/air-conditioning specialty.

Cross-check the individual technician's credentials where applicable. An old directory listing or self-posted license number is not proof of current standing.

Open the official Virginia license lookup ↗

Not a complete enforcement audit: Only three BBB complaint pages were successfully verified. “Not checked” does not mean “zero complaints.”

Before you schedule

My recommended call order

1. Repair-first local firms

Warren, Enclave, and Lin Jarrett — ask each for the diagnostic fee and a repair-versus-replacement opinion.

2. Larger sample and second bid

AirMaxx, AF1, Armstrong — confirm who will personally perform the service, not just the brand reputation.

3. Match the actual problem

Older oil/boiler/geothermal → Lin Jarrett or Waltman. Repeat failures → Warren or Enclave. High replacement quote → compare Page and Armstrong.

Order is editorial judgment based on source strength, proximity and fit — not a measured quality index.
